Privacy

Controller

Stabil Umzug (Ibrahim Abdelaal), Karlstraße 32, 45329 Essen. Email: info.stabilumzug@gmail.com, phone: 0163 9688190.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, the host processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time in server logs to deliver the pages and keep them secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Our server at Google

For the functions of this website (visitor counting, route calculation, requests, photos, appointment booking, chat assistant, customer area) we run a program at Google (Google Apps Script) and store the details in a Google spreadsheet in our Google account. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. When you use one of these functions, your browser connects directly to Google servers, so Google technically receives your IP address. We do not store IP addresses ourselves. A transfer to the USA cannot be ruled out; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Cookies and visitor counting

We use no cookies and no advertising or analytics services. Fonts and scripts load from our own domain. To see how the website is used, your browser sends the page, the kind of source (e.g. “Google”, “WhatsApp” or “direct”), the device type (phone, tablet, computer) and the language to our server at Google on each page view. We keep only daily totals without IP address that cannot be traced back to you, and delete them after 13 months (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Moving planner

The planner first keeps your entries only in your browser's local storage on this device (no cookies) so you can continue later. You can delete the draft at any time with “Delete draft and start over” or in your browser settings. Only when you click “Send request” do we transfer your details (contact details, addresses, access, items and the page or campaign that brought you to us) to our server and process them to prepare a quote (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). With “Continue on another device” we store your draft for 30 days with your consent and then delete it automatically (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you send the summary yourself via WhatsApp or email, your provider's privacy terms apply as well.

Route calculation

With “Calculate distance” and “Show route and map” we send the two places to Google Maps via our server to calculate distance, drive time and a map. For our statistics we store per calculation only time, postcode and town of start and destination, distance, page and campaign – no street, no contact details – and delete these entries after 6 months (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Photos

You can upload photos with a request (up to 10). Your browser shrinks them before sending; we receive them by email in our Google mailbox, keep them in our Google Drive and use them only to plan your move (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Please avoid photographing people or documents.

Appointment booking

When you book an appointment, we store its type, day and time as well as your name, phone, email, address and message and send a confirmation email to you and to us (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Chat assistant

The chat assistant answers general questions. Your message goes to our server; if AI answers are switched on there, the text is sent to Google (Gemini API) to generate the answer. We do not store chat messages. Please do not enter addresses, phone numbers or other personal details in the chat – use the request form or appointment booking for that (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Customer area “My move”

With your personal link you see the status and quote of your move. If you confirm an order there with your signature, we store the name, time and the image of the signature with your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Ticked checklist items are stored only by your browser on this device. Do not share the link.

Contact

If you call us or write via WhatsApp or email, we process your details to handle the request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). WhatsApp's own terms apply as well.

Storage period

Requests, photos and appointments that do not lead to an order are deleted after 6 months. For orders we keep quotes, invoices and accounting records as long as the law requires (usually 6 to 10 years under § 147 AO and § 257 HGB).

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time for the future. Write to us at info.stabilumzug@gmail.com. You can also complain to a supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia, Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany.
