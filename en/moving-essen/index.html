Moving in Essen

We are based in Essen and know the routes across the Ruhr area.

Whether an old building in Rüttenscheid, a tower block in the north or a house in the south: access decides time and crew. So we ask about floor, lift, stairs and parking.

What often helps in Essen

  • Apply early for a parking permit zone, especially in dense streets.
  • State narrow stairwells in old buildings in the planner so the crew plans accordingly.
  • Register within the deadline after moving in.
