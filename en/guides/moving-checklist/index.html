General guidance, not legal or official advice. Rules differ from city to city and can change. Check with the responsible office.

About 8 to 12 weeks before

  • Check your lease: the notice period for tenants is usually three months. Your contract decides.
  • Measure the new flat: rooms, doors, stairwell, kitchen connections. Here is how.
  • Take stock: what moves, what is sold or thrown away? The moving planner helps, and the inventory guide explains what matters.
  • Decide on the kitchen: take it, buy the previous tenant's or order a new one. New kitchens often take several weeks, see kitchen and moving in.
  • Get quotes and reserve a date.

About 4 to 6 weeks before

  • Book the city's bulky-waste collection, often needed weeks in advance. More here.
  • Apply for a no-parking zone, for both the old and new address. More here.
  • Sort out time off and help from friends.
  • Transfer or cancel contracts: electricity, gas, internet, insurance. Request internet especially early; activation can take weeks.
  • Set up mail forwarding.

One to two weeks before

  • Start packing: cellar, guest room and books first, the kitchen last. Which boxes for what.
  • Label boxes by destination room and keep a packing list.
  • Set aside bags for screws and fittings for taking furniture apart.

Last week

  • Pack and label the remaining boxes room by room.
  • Defrost the fridge, disconnect the washing machine.
  • Carry valuables and key documents yourself.
  • Photograph the meter readings.

Moving day and after

  • Handover record with the landlord, in the old and the new flat.
  • Count and hand over keys.
  • Report visible damage immediately at the handover. More on insurance.
  • Register at the residents' registration office, normally within two weeks. Details.
  • Update your address with bank, employer and insurers.
