Moving boxes: which size for which contents

The right box depends on what goes in it. With one simple rule and a packing list, less goes wrong when packing and unpacking.

The key rule: heavy in small, light in large

Heavy things such as books go into small boxes, light and bulky things such as bedding into large ones. Mixing this up risks split boxes and sore backs. It is the most common packing mistake.

Approximate planning values, not measurements. Your furniture and household can differ considerably. The sizes are common market sizes; suppliers differ by a few centimetres.

The packing list

Give each box a number and note:

  • Destination room in the new home.
  • Contents in a few words.
  • Fragile: yes or no.
  • Who packed it.

Label by destination room, not by old room. Then the helpers carry each box straight to the right place, and unloading is noticeably faster.

When to start

Usually start one to two weeks before the move. Rarely used rooms come first: cellar, guest room, books. Pack the kitchen last. The guide on moving volume shows roughly how many boxes you need.

In the moving planner

In the moving planner you can add boxes as your own items with dimensions, for example "book box 40 × 30 × 30", or include them in a pasted list. That way they count towards the volume estimate.
