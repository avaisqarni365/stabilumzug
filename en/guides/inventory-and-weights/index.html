Why the list decides so much

The inventory gives the volume, the number of boxes, the size of the van and the number of helpers. Forgotten items or guessed weights lead to a quote that is too low or a van that is too small. For each item, note the room, whether it moves and whether it can be taken apart.

What is often forgotten

Kitchen (pots, crockery, cutlery, small appliances), bathroom, clothes, bedding, books, toys, tools, bicycles, plants, cellar contents and documents. Whatever does not move goes on a separate list for selling, donating or bulky waste.

What to carry yourself

Important documents, electronics such as laptops, and jewellery do not belong on the van. Take these with you.

In the moving planner

In the moving planner you record your belongings room by room: from the catalogue, with a preset for your household size, as a quick guess per room or as a pasted list. You can add your own items with dimensions.
