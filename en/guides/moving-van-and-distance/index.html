Moving van and distance: which type of move suits you

From a self-move to a full-service move: the type of move decides what you do yourself. The distance decides how long the day gets.

Four types of move

  • Self-move: you rent a van or truck and carry everything yourself, with friends or paid helpers.
  • Van with driver and helpers: the moving company provides the vehicle and carriers; you pack.
  • Full-service move: the company packs, takes furniture apart, transports, reassembles and can also fit the kitchen.
  • Part-load (Beiladung): for small volumes or long distances, your goods share a truck with other customers.

The individual services we offer are listed under services.

What a good quote contains

Volume, number of helpers, floors and carry distance at both addresses, whether packing material is included and the cancellation terms.

The route has several legs

What counts is road distance along the real route, not the straight line.

LegFrom → toWhy it matters
PositioningDepot → old addressDriving time and arrival time
TransportOld address → new addressMain distance with a full load
ReturnNew address → depotPart of the round trip
Side tripsOld address → recycling centre or storageDisposal, storage or a second load
Carry distanceVan → front door at each addressNot kilometres but metres, and so time

Local, regional or long distance

BandApprox. distanceWhat it means for planning
Localunder 50 kmUsually one day; one van can make several trips
Regional50–300 kmDrivers' working hours apply; often an early start
Long distanceover 300 kmOvernight stay for the crew; unloading the next day

Approximate planning values, not measurements. Your furniture and household can differ considerably. The band limits are guide values.

Two more points: many cities have low-emission zones (Umweltzonen) that restrict older vehicles. And for local moves, traffic and time of day around both addresses often matter more than the kilometres. On long-distance moves the unloading day changes, and so does the no-parking zone at the destination.

In the moving planner

In the moving planner you enter both addresses and the planner works out the distance. Together with the volume, it estimates the vehicle, crew and hours.
