Applying for a parking permit zone

How to have the moving van close to the door on moving day.

General guidance, not legal or official advice. Rules and deadlines can change; check with the responsible authority.

Why a parking permit zone?

A moving van is long and heavy. Far from the entrance, it lengthens the day. A signed zone keeps the space free.

How does it work?

  • You apply to the traffic authority of your city, often online or in writing.
  • Give the address, date, length of the zone and time period.
  • Processing time and fee differ by city. Ask early.
  • Signs usually go up several days before, often at least 72 hours. Check your city's rule.
  • The zone must be visible to others. If someone parks there anyway, the authority or a towing service can help.

We are happy to help with the application and the signs. Tell us in the planner that you want the zone.
