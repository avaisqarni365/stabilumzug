Four routes to a kitchen

Many rented flats have no fitted kitchen (Einbauküche). So you decide where your kitchen comes from. Each choice changes the move.

OptionRouteApprox. lead timeWhat it means for the move
Take the old kitchenDismantle, move, reinstallDays to weeks, plus a fitterDismantling, wrapping, transport, refitting with a specialist fitter
Buy the previous tenant's kitchen (Ablöse)Private sale at the new flatImmediateOften nothing; the kitchen already fits
Buy a new kitchenKitchen studio, furniture store, manufacturer or online plannerSeveral weeksReceive the delivery; coordinate delivery and fitter dates
Buy a used kitchenOnline classifieds or specialist portalsDaysDismantle at the seller, transport, reinstall

Approximate planning values, not measurements. Your furniture and household can differ considerably. Delivery times depend on the supplier.

Planning a kitchen in five steps

  1. Measure walls, ceiling height, windows and doors; mark water, drain, power and gas points. See measuring your new home.
  2. Plan the layout: cabinet runs, appliances, worktop.
  3. Choose cabinets, appliances and worktop; confirm delivery and fitting dates.
  4. Have the cabinets fitted. Licensed trades connect water, electricity and gas.
  5. Inspect, sign off and dispose of the packaging.

Utilities, internet and handover

  • Electricity, gas and water: set a start date and note meter readings at both addresses.
  • Request internet early. Activation can take days or weeks.
  • Order new furniture against your room measurements and have it delivered after the old furniture is in.
  • Handover record (Übergabeprotokoll): note condition and meter readings in both homes. It protects your deposit.

Everything is linked: if the kitchen is late or the internet is missing, you cannot settle in, even when the van arrives on time. For help with dismantling and fitting kitchens, see our services.

In the moving planner

In the moving planner you list kitchen units like other furniture, or as your own items with dimensions. From the summary sent by WhatsApp or e-mail, we can see whether a kitchen is moving.
