How it works

Done in four steps.

    Request

  2. 02

    Survey and quote

Common questions

Briefly answered.

How much does a household clearance in Essen cost?

It depends on the size of the flat, the amount of contents, access and disposal costs. After a survey you receive a free, personal quote.

Do I need to be there during the clearance?

Not necessarily. What matters is that it is clear beforehand which items are being kept and how we get the key. We agree this together.

What does swept clean mean?

The rooms are emptied and roughly swept. Whether and to what extent we do this is agreed with you in advance.
