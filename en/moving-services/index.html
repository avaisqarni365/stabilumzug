How it works

Done in four steps.

    Request

    Tell us about your move via form, phone or WhatsApp – or in detail with our moving planner.

  2. 02

    Survey and quote

Common questions

Briefly answered.

How much does a move in Essen cost?

It mainly depends on the volume of your belongings, the distance, the floors involved and extras such as furniture assembly. We give you a free, personal quote. Read more in our guide to moving costs.

How early should I plan my move?

The earlier the better – dates at the end of the month and in summer are in high demand. Our moving checklist helps you plan the timeline.

Do you also do long-distance moves?

Yes. As well as moves in Essen and the Ruhr area, we handle moves throughout Germany.

How do I get a free quote?

Fill in our short quote form or call or WhatsApp us on 0163 9688190. For detailed planning you can also use the moving planner.
