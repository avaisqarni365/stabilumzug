Common questions

Briefly answered.

How much does furniture transport in Essen cost?

It depends on the number, size and weight of the items, the distance and access at both addresses. You receive a free, personal quote.

Do you collect furniture from shops or private sellers?

Yes. We collect furniture from retailers or private sellers and deliver it to you. You arrange the collection time with the seller.

Do you help with carrying?

Yes, carrying help is included by arrangement – including to upper floors without a lift.

How do I get a free quote?

Use our short quote form or call or WhatsApp us on 0163 9688190. Dimensions and photos of the furniture help us.
