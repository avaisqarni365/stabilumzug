Common questions

Moving in Bochum: briefly answered.

What does a move in Bochum cost?

The price depends on the amount, floor, lift, carrying distance and distance. We calculate every line personally and send you the free quote within 24 hours.

How quickly can you be in Bochum?

From our depot at Karlstraße 32 it is 23 km, about 35 minutes by car according to Google Maps – longer in rush hour.

Do you also do clear-outs and transport in Bochum?

Yes. Besides moves we clear cellars, attics and whole flats and transport single pieces of furniture – in Bochum and across the Ruhr area.

Do you come to Bochum for a viewing?

Yes, free and non-binding. Book a free time online – on site in Bochum or by video. The confirmation arrives by email right away.

Do I need a no-parking zone in Bochum?

It depends on the street: if there is no room for the van in front of the house, it almost always pays off. The City of Bochum is responsible – we advise you and apply for it on request.
