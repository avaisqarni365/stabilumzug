Services

What we take care of.

Pick-up at the furniture shop, delivery into your home and assembly – all from one provider.

Pick-up at the furniture shop

You buy and pay, we collect – at the goods issue or pick-up point, during the shop's opening hours.

Delivery into your home

We carry everything to the right floor and room, even without a lift.

Furniture assembly

We assemble wardrobes, beds, chests of drawers, shelves and tables following the instructions and fix them to the wall if you wish.

Furniture ordered online

If your order is only delivered to the kerb, we carry the parcels up, unpack and assemble.

Second-hand and private purchases

We collect used furniture from private sellers, take it apart there and put it back together at your home.

Packaging taken away

We take boxes, film and polystyrene with us if you wish, so your home is ready to use straight away.

Common questions

Briefly answered.

Do you also collect from IKEA?

Yes. We collect from IKEA, ROLLER, POCO, XXXLutz, Ostermann and any other furniture shop. You buy and pay yourself; we need the pick-up slip or order number. We are not a partner of these shops but collect on your behalf.

What do pick-up and assembly cost?

It depends on distance, amount, floor and assembly time. You get a free, personal quote in advance – via the quote form, phone or WhatsApp.

Do I have to be there for the pick-up?

That depends on the shop. Often the pick-up slip and a short authorisation are enough. We check with you in advance what is needed.

How long does assembly take?

The guide values on this page are for two people. Large wardrobe systems and furniture with many drawers take longer; the exact time is in your quote.

Do you also assemble kitchens?

Yes, kitchen assembly is part of our extra services.
